Freight Information & Support

We believe reliable logistics begins with clear communication. Our Frequently Asked Questions section provides practical information about freight transportation, shipment tracking, documentation, claims, delivery processes, and operational policies.

Whether you are planning a single shipment or managing an ongoing transportation program, these answers provide an overview of how our logistics services work and what you can expect throughout the transportation lifecycle.

01

What types of freight do you transport?

We support a broad range of commercial and industrial freight requirements, including FMCG and retail goods, pharmaceutical products, automotive components, construction materials, steel, industrial equipment, e-commerce shipments, and other commercial cargo.

Vehicle selection and transportation arrangements are determined based on factors such as cargo type, weight, dimensions, volume, destination, route, handling requirements, and delivery schedule.

02

Do you provide Pan-India transportation services?

Yes. Our transportation network is designed to support Pan-India freight movement across 28 states, supported by a network of 120+ regional transshipment hubs. This enables connectivity between manufacturing facilities, warehouses, distribution centres, fulfillment hubs, industrial locations, and regional markets across major transportation corridors.

03

How do I book a freight shipment?

Shipment bookings can be initiated by sharing the required transportation details with our logistics team. These typically include:

  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Cargo type and description
  • Approximate weight and volume
  • Number of packages or units
  • Required vehicle type, where applicable
  • Preferred pickup date
  • Delivery timeline
  • Special handling or transportation requirements
04

How is the appropriate vehicle selected for my shipment?

Vehicle selection depends on cargo characteristics, payload requirements, dimensions, route, distance, loading conditions, and delivery requirements. Our fleet includes high-capacity commercial transportation solutions suitable for different freight categories, with specialized configurations selected for heavy machinery, structural materials, and temperature-sensitive products where appropriate.

05

Can I track my shipment during transit?

Shipment tracking and visibility may be available depending on the service and transportation arrangement. Where tracking is enabled, shipment movement can be monitored through the applicable tracking mechanism and operational updates. For enterprise and scheduled transportation programs, our logistics team can also provide appropriate shipment-status communication throughout the transportation cycle.

06

What information is required to track a shipment?

Typically, shipment tracking requires an applicable consignment number, shipment reference number, booking reference, or other identification details provided at the time of booking. Providing the correct reference information allows our team to identify the shipment and provide the relevant transportation status.

07

What happens if my shipment is delayed?

While we work to maintain agreed transportation schedules, delays can occasionally occur due to weather conditions, traffic restrictions, road conditions, regulatory checks, unforeseen operational disruptions, or other circumstances beyond direct operational control. Where a delay occurs, our team works to assess the situation, coordinate the next steps, and provide relevant status updates.

08

How do you handle damaged or missing freight?

We follow established operational procedures for handling reports of damage, shortage, loss, or other freight-related discrepancies. Clients should report any visible damage or discrepancy as soon as possible and provide the relevant shipment documentation and supporting information for review according to the applicable transportation terms and claims process.

09

How do I file a freight claim?

A claim should generally be raised through the designated claims or customer-service channel with relevant shipment details and supporting documentation.

  • Consignment or shipment reference
  • Invoice or commercial documentation
  • Delivery documentation
  • Photographs of damaged cargo or packaging
  • Details of shortage or discrepancy
  • Proof of value, where applicable
  • Relevant inspection or supporting reports
10

Is there a time limit for reporting damaged or missing cargo?

Reporting timelines may vary depending on the applicable service agreement, transportation terms, cargo category, and nature of the claim. Customers are therefore advised to report damage, shortage, loss, or delivery discrepancies as soon as they are identified and retain all relevant documentation and evidence.

11

Do you provide temperature-controlled transportation?

Yes. Temperature-controlled transportation solutions can be provided for suitable pharmaceutical, healthcare, and other temperature-sensitive cargo requirements. Reefer transportation may include temperature monitoring and data logging capabilities depending on the specific service arrangement and cargo requirements.

12

How is temperature-sensitive cargo monitored?

For applicable reefer shipments, temperature conditions can be monitored during transportation using suitable monitoring and data-logging systems. Specific temperature ranges, monitoring procedures, reporting formats, and compliance requirements should be established according to the product and applicable service agreement before transportation begins.

13

Can you transport heavy or oversized cargo?

We support transportation requirements involving heavy and industrial cargo, subject to vehicle suitability, cargo dimensions, weight, route feasibility, regulatory requirements, and applicable transportation restrictions. Suitable configurations may include flatbed trailers, multi-axle vehicles, and other heavy-duty commercial transportation solutions where appropriate.

14

Do you provide transportation for recurring or scheduled shipments?

Yes. Our logistics capabilities can support recurring transportation programs for businesses with regular freight movement requirements. Scheduled transportation can be structured around shipment frequency, route requirements, cargo volume, delivery windows, vehicle capacity, and regional distribution patterns.

15

Can transportation capacity be scaled during peak demand?

Our fleet and network are designed to support changing transportation requirements. Additional capacity may be planned around seasonal demand, promotional periods, project requirements, business expansion, peak e-commerce volumes, or other temporary increases in freight movement. Capacity availability remains subject to route, vehicle type, timing, and operational requirements.

16

What documentation is required for freight transportation?

Documentation requirements depend on the cargo type, origin and destination, applicable regulations, and nature of the shipment. Customers may be required to provide appropriate commercial, transportation, tax, regulatory, or product-specific documentation before dispatch. Our logistics team can advise on the documentation required for the applicable transportation service.

17

How is freight secured during transportation?

Cargo handling and securing procedures are determined according to the nature, size, weight, packaging, and configuration of the freight. For industrial and heavy cargo, appropriate loading and securement practices are particularly important to support safe transportation and minimize movement or damage during transit.

18

Do you provide dedicated vehicles?

Dedicated vehicle arrangements may be available for clients requiring exclusive transportation capacity for specific routes, projects, distribution programs, or operational schedules. Dedicated solutions can be structured around the client's volume, frequency, route network, vehicle requirements, and service expectations.

19

What areas and routes do you cover?

Our network is designed to provide broad connectivity across India, including major industrial clusters, commercial centres, manufacturing regions, distribution markets, logistics hubs, and inter-state transportation corridors. Specific route availability depends on the cargo requirement, vehicle type, origin, destination, and scheduling requirements.

20

How can I get a transportation quotation?

To request a quotation, provide our team with the key details of your transportation requirement, including pickup and delivery locations, cargo description, weight or volume, shipment frequency, preferred vehicle type, and required timeline. Our team can evaluate the requirement and provide an appropriate transportation proposal based on the applicable route and service requirements.
