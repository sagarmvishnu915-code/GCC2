Our Vision & Value

Moving Commerce. Connecting Industries. Supporting India's Growth Story.

For economies to grow, goods must move. Factories must receive raw materials. Retailers must replenish inventory. Manufacturers must deliver products. Exporters must reach global markets. Importers must receive critical supplies. Infrastructure projects must obtain equipment. Businesses must maintain uninterrupted supply chains. Behind every successful economy lies an efficient logistics ecosystem.

For nearly two decades, Goods Carrying Cargo Private Limited has operated within this critical sector, contributing to the movement of goods, the coordination of supply chains, and the efficient flow of commerce across regions and industries.

Under the leadership and strategic guidance of Dr. Vikas Kumar Modi, the company became part of a sector that directly influences productivity, economic development, industrial growth, and national competitiveness.

"The story of Goods Carrying Cargo Private Limited is therefore not simply about transportation. It is about enabling economic activity. It is about supporting businesses. It is about facilitating trade. And ultimately, it is about contributing to the growth of India."

OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Logistics Solutions

From express transportation and international movement to rail, shipping and storage solutions, Goods Carrying Cargo provides logistics services designed to support the changing requirements of modern businesses and supply chains.

Surface Express

Reliable road transportation for domestic and time-sensitive consignments. Surface Express solutions are suitable for commercial goods, FMCG, retail products, industrial materials, automotive components and other cargo requiring dependable road connectivity.

  • Domestic road transportation
  • Time-sensitive consignments
  • Commercial and industrial cargo

Domestic Air Express

Faster domestic transportation for shipments where delivery time is critical. This service is suitable for urgent documents, samples, critical components, high-value products and other time-sensitive cargo.

  • Faster domestic movement
  • Urgent shipments
  • Documents, samples and critical cargo

International Air Express

Time-sensitive international transportation solutions for businesses requiring faster cross-border movement. Suitable for documents, samples, commercial shipments and other urgent consignments.

  • International express movement
  • Cross-border shipments
  • Commercial and urgent cargo

Rail Express

Efficient rail transportation for suitable commercial and bulk cargo moving over longer distances. Rail Express provides an alternative transportation mode for businesses with planned long-distance freight requirements.

  • Long-distance freight movement
  • Commercial and bulk cargo
  • Multimodal transportation support

Shipping

Shipping solutions for businesses requiring domestic or international maritime transportation. Suitable for larger consignments, planned cargo movement and long-distance freight requirements.

  • Domestic and international movement
  • Large-volume consignments
  • Long-distance freight solutions

Storage

Secure storage and warehousing solutions that help businesses manage inventory between transportation stages. Storage requirements can be planned according to cargo type, volume, duration and distribution needs.

  • Secure cargo storage
  • Inventory holding solutions
  • Distribution support
