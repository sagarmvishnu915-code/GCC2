Legal & Operational Terms

Responsible Goods Carriage and Conditions of Service

At GOODS CARRYING CARGO PRIVATE LIMITED, our carriage policy outlines the legal framework, terms of engagement, and operational responsibilities that govern the transportation of goods across our national network. Built upon three decades of industry experience, this policy ensures absolute clarity between our organization and our valued clients regarding freight acceptance, liabilities, and safety compliance.

We operate in strict adherence to statutory transport laws, mandating accurate cargo declarations, legal e-way documentation, and proper weight specifications for every consignment. Our objective is to maintain a secure, lawful, and predictable transportation environment that protects both customer assets and carrier operations.

Please review the core principles of our carriage policy below to ensure a smooth, compliant, and transparent shipping experience.

Key Terms

Core Elements of Our Carriage Policy

Our terms of carriage are structured to uphold safety, legality, and mutual accountability:

Accurate Freight Declaration

Shippers must provide precise details regarding cargo weight, dimensions, nature of goods, and declared value.

Prohibited & Restricted Goods

Strict prohibition of hazardous, illegal, inflammable, or restricted materials not permitted by law for general transport.

Carrier Liability Guidelines

Defined terms of liability covering standard transit risks, force majeure events, and recommended insurance coverage.

Consignment Note (LR) Terms

The Lorry Receipt (LR) acts as the official contract of carriage, binding both parties to the stated terms and conditions.

Packaging Responsibility

The shipper is responsible for industrial-grade internal and external packaging to endure standard transit handling.

Delivery & Demurrage Terms

Guidelines for timely delivery, cargo collection windows, and applicable holding charges for delayed pickups.

Our Commitment to Lawful & Secure Carriage

By enforcing a standardized carriage policy, we protect the integrity of our logistics operations and ensure fair, dependable service for every customer. We encourage all clients to review these guidelines before dispatching consignments.
